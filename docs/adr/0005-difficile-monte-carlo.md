# Difficile plays cards by Monte-Carlo determinisation, with Moyen as its playout policy

Difficile keeps Moyen's bidding, king call, écart and poignée, and only chooses its cards differently. For each card it may play, it draws sampled deals (full distributions of the hidden cards) that fit everything its seat knows: cards played, colours others lack, shown poignées and écart trumps, the revealed chien, and a known partner. In each sampled deal it plays the card, has Moyen play the rest of the deal at every seat, and keeps the card with the best average final score for its seat. The same sampled deals judge every card. Their number is fixed for a given number of playable cards (20, or fewer so that at most 200 deals are played out per decision), so a seed always gives the same decision and a decision stays well under the room server's 1 s target. A time budget was rejected: it varies with the machine, and tests could not reproduce its decisions.

Playing out a sampled deal needs the whole history of the deal, which the redacted view does not hold (only the last trick). So the bot memory records every completed trick, and the room server rebuilds it from the public tricks for a bot standing in mid-deal. The engine builds a validated deal in play from that history and one sampled distribution (`createDealInPlay`), so the bots never assemble engine state by hand.

## Consequences

- In 5 players Difficile plays like Moyen until the partner is known. Each sampled deal settles the partner differently, and choosing a card that is best in each such sampled deal lost about 12 points a deal against Moyen. Waiting turned that into a gain (benchmark, paired deals: +12 at 3 players, +22 at 4, +9 at 5).
- Sampled deals do not use every inference the table allows. Levers if Difficile needs to be stronger: the bidding (a taker is likely strong), an Excuse shown in a poignée (its owner has no other trump), trumps shown in the écart (the taker had nothing else to discard).
- The benchmark plays each deal twice on the same cards, once with Moyen in Difficile's seats, because the score of single deals is too noisy. It takes minutes and runs on demand (`pnpm --filter @tarot/bots bench`), not in the normal test suite.
